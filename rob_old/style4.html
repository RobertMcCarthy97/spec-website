// independent AI behaviour measurement

If we can't measure AI character, we can't govern it.

We quantify how AI systems behave under realistic conditions — and check those measurements against developers' published constitutions and model specs.

CONTEXT

Frontier labs now publish the intended values and behaviours of their systems in “AI constitutions” and “model specs.” As these systems become more powerful and embedded in society, their behaviour will shape the world we live in.

To avoid societal-scale risks, we must decide which behaviours we want and verify that AIs reliably exhibit them.
