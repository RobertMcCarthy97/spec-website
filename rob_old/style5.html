In the public interest

The behaviour of AI will shape the society we live in.

We are an independent organisation measuring how AI systems behave — and verifying whether they uphold the values their developers promise.

Our mission Get in touch

Deciding which behaviours we want — and verifying them.

Frontier AI labs have begun to define the intended values and behaviours of their systems in public-facing “AI constitutions” and “model specs.” As these systems become more powerful and deeply integrated into society, their character will increasingly shape the world we live in.

To avoid societal-scale risks from future AI, we must decide which behaviours we want and verify that AIs reliably exhibit them.

What we do

Measure

Develop methods to measure and understand key AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions.

Verify

Independently evaluate whether AIs behave in line with their public constitution or model spec.

Collaborate

Work with developers to encourage AI propensities that are beneficial for society.

Questions we work on

  • What red lines should we draw for AI behaviours?
  • What goals should highly autonomous agents pursue?
  • How should agents handle rare, high-stakes dilemmas?
  • How should AIs behave when training their successors?
