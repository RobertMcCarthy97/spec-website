Mission Approach People

Independent evaluation of AI behaviour

Measuring whether AI systems behave as they are meant to.

We develop rigorous methods to measure AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions, and independently verify whether systems behave in line with their developers' stated values.

The problem

Frontier AI labs now publish the intended values and behaviours of their systems in public “AI constitutions” and “model specs.” As these systems grow more powerful and more deeply integrated into society, their character will increasingly shape the world we live in.

To avoid societal-scale risks, we must decide which behaviours we want — and verify that AIs reliably exhibit them.

What we do

Measure

Develop methods to measure and understand key AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions.

Verify

Independently evaluate whether AIs behave in line with their public-facing constitution or model spec.

Collaborate

Work with developers to encourage the design of AI propensities that are beneficial for society.

Questions we work on

  • What red lines should we draw for AI behaviours?
  • What goals should highly autonomous agents pursue?
  • How should agents handle rare, high-stakes ethical dilemmas?
  • How should AIs behave when training their successors?
