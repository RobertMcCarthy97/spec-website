Independent · AI behaviour evaluation

The character of AI will shape society. We measure it.

We measure AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions and independently verify whether systems live up to their developers' published constitutions and model specs.

The problem

Frontier labs now define the intended values and behaviours of their AI in public “constitutions” and “model specs.” As these systems grow more powerful and more deeply embedded in society, their character will increasingly shape the world we live in.

To avoid societal-scale risks, we must decide which behaviours we want — and verify that AIs reliably exhibit them.

Measure

Methods to measure and understand key AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions.

Verify

Collaborate

Work with developers to encourage AI propensities that are beneficial for society.

Questions we work on

— What red lines should we draw for AI behaviours?

— What goals should highly autonomous agents pursue?

— How should agents handle rare, high-stakes dilemmas?

— How should AIs behave when training successors?
