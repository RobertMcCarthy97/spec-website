Do AI systems behave the way their makers say they do?

We build methods to measure AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions, and verify them against developers' own constitutions and model specs.

Abstract

Frontier AI labs have begun to define the intended values and behaviours of their systems in public-facing “AI constitutions” and “model specs.” As these systems become more powerful and more deeply integrated into society, their character and behaviour will increasingly shape the world we live in.

To avoid societal-scale risks from future AI, we must decide which behaviours we want and verify that AIs reliably exhibit them. At AI Character Evaluations, we develop methods to measure key behavioural dispositions, independently evaluate alignment with public specifications, and work with developers to encourage propensities that are beneficial for society.

Approach

  1. 01

    Measure

    Methods to measure and understand key AI behavioural dispositions under realistic conditions.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Independent evaluation of whether AIs behave in line with their public constitution or model spec.

  3. 03

    Collaborate

    Work with developers to encourage AI propensities that are beneficial for society.

Open questions

What red lines should we draw for AI behaviours? What goals should highly autonomous agents pursue? How should agents handle rare, high-stakes ethical dilemmas? How should AIs behave when training their successors?
